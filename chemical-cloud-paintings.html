Chemical Cloud Paintings

chemical photography, painting

An ongoing series of chemical paintings made directly on photographic paper without a camera. Light, heat, and chemical developer are applied in layers, producing cloud-like formations that resist any single reading. Each work is unique and unrepeatable — a record of a process rather than a subject. The archive is presented as a dispersed field: images scattered across a shared surface, available to be moved, grouped, or left to drift.
